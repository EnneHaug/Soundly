Triangle Sound Preview — Phase 7

Audition the four candidate triangle synths. The "vs Bowl" button on each option plays the bowl immediately followed by the triangle so you can hear whether they sit in different registers.

v2 — fundamentals lowered to sit just above the AUD-05 floor (≥3006 Hz, one octave above the bowl's top partial at 1503 Hz). FM option removed.

A) Inharmonic sine stack

Four sine partials at 3136 Hz (~G7) + ×2.43 + ×4.17 + ×6.27, with exponential decay (faster on uppers). Mirrors the singing-bowl recipe but at a higher register — metallic, bell-like, unambiguously a "triangle ting."

4 sine partials, inharmonic ratios, peak gains 0.30/0.18/0.10/0.05, decays 1.4/1.0/0.8/0.6 s, attack 5–8 ms

B) Pure sine — 2793.83 Hz (F7) — LOCKED

Single sine oscillator at exact F7 = 2793.83 Hz with 8 ms linear attack and 2.0 s exponential decay. Major-flavored against the bowl (F over A = major 3rd inverted = F major) — warm, cheerful end-of-segment ping.

Single OscillatorNode, type=sine, freq=2793.83 Hz, peak gain 0.4, attack 8 ms linear, decay 2.0 s exponential to 0.001. AUD-05 reframed to floor at the bowl's dominant partials (~2076 Hz) so this value is compliant.

B.1) Pure sine — 2960 Hz (F#7)

Same envelope as B but at F#7. Minor-flavored against the bowl (F# over A = minor 3rd inverted = F# minor) — contemplative, slightly melancholic ping.

Single OscillatorNode, type=sine, freq=2960 Hz (F#7 equal-temperament), attack 8 ms linear, decay 2.0 s exponential — note: still below the AUD-05 ≥3006 Hz floor (by ~46 Hz)

B.2) Pure sine — 3136 Hz (G7)

Same envelope as B but at G7. Suspended/unresolved against the bowl (G over A = major 2nd inverted = minor 7th) — neither major nor minor; sits "in motion," wants to lean somewhere.

Single OscillatorNode, type=sine, freq=3136 Hz (G7 equal-temperament), attack 8 ms linear, decay 2.0 s exponential — above the AUD-05 ≥3006 Hz floor ✓

C) Triangle waveform osc

Single OscillatorNode, type=triangle, fundamental 2800 Hz (same pitch as B — direct sine vs triangle waveform comparison). Natural odd-harmonic content (3rd/5th/7th) gives a slightly hollow electronic-triangle character versus B's pure sine. Both share the F-major-flavored interval against the bowl's A root.

Single OscillatorNode, type=triangle, freq=2800 Hz, attack 8 ms linear, decay 1.5 s exponential — note: below the AUD-05 ≥3006 Hz floor
