Questions

Will Soundly actually wake me up?

Yes — if you use it the way it's designed. The first phase plays soft sounds; the second adds vibration on devices that support it; the third ramps to full volume. The escalation is the safety net. For deep sleepers, keep the device near you and the screen on. (iPhone has limitations — see below.)

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, Soundly runs entirely on your device — no server calls during an alarm. The landing page is online; the app itself is offline-capable.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Soundly is free and has no ads, no accounts, no tracking. It will not start charging later.

Where's my data?

Nowhere — Soundly stores no settings between sessions and sends nothing to a server. Custom alarms can be shared via link (which encodes the alarm into the URL itself); the link is the only "storage."

Can I customize the alarm sounds and timing?

Yes. The Custom composer lets you stack any number of segments, pick a sound per segment (gentle chime, triangle ping, or full alarm), and set the duration of each.

Why is there no sign-in?

By design. Soundly is meant to be the simplest possible alarm — install, set, sleep. No accounts to recover, no email to confirm, no profile to maintain.

If you're on iPhone

We wish Soundly worked perfectly on iPhone's locked screen — but iOS blocks PWA audio in that state. We're being honest so you can decide if Soundly fits your iPhone use.

Here's how to use Soundly reliably on iPhone: install to your home screen, plug in or keep the screen on while the alarm is armed, and use it as a nap timer or focus timer where you'll be near the phone.
